Research Analyst
New Organizing

Based in Michigan

 

About the organization:
SEIU Healthcare Michigan (HCMI) is a dynamic labor union and the largest healthcare worker union in the state, representing over 50,000 long-term care and hospital workers. HCMI members are dedicated to raising standards for healthcare workers and ensuring access to affordable healthcare for all Michiganders. As part of the two-million-member Service Employees International Union (SEIU), we believe in valuing all workers and respecting all people, fostering thriving communities, and leaving a more equitable world for future generations. We work to build a strong, vibrant, multi-racial, multi- generational labor movement that centers worker power as the vehicle to create good union jobs and healthy communities for all who call Michigan home.

About the position:
The Research Analyst provides research support for external SEIU Healthcare Michigan’s campaigns to bring more healthcare workers into the union through elections, card check, recognition or other methods. The Research Analyst will develop an understanding and analysis of industry and employers to support organizing goals of the department and build power and improve standards for all healthcare workers. Campaigns can include union organizing campaigns, worker issue campaigns, and public policy campaigns, including developing or supporting legislation. This position reports to the External Organizing Director.

Job Qualifications:

    A minimum of one year’s experience as a researcher or doing research support for a labor union or organization
    Strong commitment to social, economic, and racial justice
    Experience supporting corporate responsibility or accountability campaign(s) preferred
    Coursework or experience working in economics, financial analysis, and public policy analysis preferred
    The ability to learn how to conduct deep analyses of healthcare employer finances and business models is essential to the role
    The ability and/or interest to understand key employers and growth within outpatient healthcare industry
    Knowledge of or experience in the healthcare industry and/or the public sector preferred
    Knowledge of NLRB, MERC, elections/card procedures preferred 
    Willingness to work long and irregular hours as needed
    Proficiency with Excel, Word, and PowerPoint as well as good internet and database research skills
    A desire to be part of a collaborative team in the constantly developing fields of healthcare and workers’ rights  

Primary Responsibilities

    Compiles, analyzes, summarizes, and presents information on industries, companies, individuals, and issues in support of SEIU Healthcare Michigan’s program
    Gathers information through desk research as well as field research, both qualitative and quantitative – including engaging with members and soon to be members, site visits, interviews, surveys, networking, attending public meetings and industry events, and more
    Collaborates with field team extensively and creatively
    Be part of an incredible team building union density and power with a focus on corporate accountability
    Synthesizes relevant facts from a large amount of information
    Develops and implements a weekly work plan; meets deadlines
    Learns new skills and areas of expertise
    Performs other duties as required to support the union and its mission
